01Problem

Indoor agriculture lighting has been stuck on plant-grade LEDs since 2015. Diodes have crept from 2.5 to 3.1 µmol/J in a decade — a 24% gain — while energy is now the single largest line item for every commercial vertical farm in North America and the EU.

The result is a category that quietly broke. Of the eleven vertical-farm operators that raised over $50M between 2019 and 2023, seven have shut down, sold for parts, or restructured at zero equity value. Three of the survivors cite lighting cost-per-gram as the headline blocker on their next facility.

Greenhouse supplemental lighting is the same shape of problem, masked by daylight. A 12-hectare Dutch tomato greenhouse running 200 W/m² HPS supplement for 16 hours a day spends roughly €3.1M per year on electricity for photons. Swapping to current best-in-class LED bars cuts that to €2.0M. It is not enough. Operators need a step change, not another 20%.

02Solution

Lumenforge builds tunable-spectrum micro-fusion-emitter arrays — lighting fixtures that use inertial-confinement micro-pellet emitters to produce a continuously tunable photon spectrum from 380 nm to 780 nm at 24.6 µmol/J wall-plug efficiency.

That is roughly 8× the photosynthetic photon flux per watt of the best commercial horticultural LED shipping today. The output spectrum is software-defined per square meter, so growers can run a "morning blue" recipe at dawn and a "fruit-set red" recipe at noon without swapping hardware or losing yield to spectral compromise.

One MF-220 fixture replaces seven Fluence SPYDR 2p bars while drawing 380 W instead of 2,310 W. We are not selling efficiency. We are selling a new unit economic.

03How it works

Each MF-220 fixture contains a 6×6 grid of micro-fusion emitter cells. Each cell holds a deuterated polymer micro-pellet (12 mg) inside a sapphire confinement cavity. A pulsed 940 nm diode array drives the pellet into a sustained low-yield aneutronic reaction; the resulting bremsstrahlung is downconverted by a doped phosphor stack into a clean visible spectrum. A pellet lasts roughly 14,000 operating hours. Spectrum is shaped per-cell by modulating phosphor bias voltages on a 2 kHz cycle — that is what makes the spectrum software-defined.

Why now

European glasshouse electricity has settled at €0.21–€0.28/kWh post-2024, more than double the pre-Ukraine baseline. Three of the largest Dutch tomato cooperatives have publicly committed to halving lighting energy by 2028. Meanwhile, North American vertical farms that survived the 2023 cull are rebuilding around unit economics, not topline — lighting cost-per-gram is the line they all need to move.

Segments we are not chasing

Cannabis indoor (regulatory whiplash, price compression), hobby and prosumer (margin-thin, support-heavy), and research chambers (volume too low). Our wedge is commercial leafy-greens vertical farms and high-density Dutch glasshouse tomato and pepper.

07Business model

Two revenue lines, intentionally coupled.

Hardware. MF-220 ASP is $1,940 per fixture at list, $1,620 at named-account volume pricing. BOM at our Q3 production run is $612, falling to $470 at 10,000 units. Gross margin walks from 62% to 71% over the funded period. Pellet refills are sold as a $89 four-pack on 14,000-hour intervals — not a profit center, a lock-in.

Spectrum Cloud (recurring). Every fixture phones home for recipe updates, yield telemetry, and a managed library of validated spectra by crop and growth stage. $14/fixture/month, billed annually. A 10,000-fixture customer pays $1.68M/year for software on top of their hardware. Spectrum Cloud carries 84% gross margin and is the line that gets us to a software multiple at exit.

Year-3 mix target: 64% hardware, 36% recurring, blended 71% GM.

08Competition

The horticultural lighting market is consolidated around three serious incumbents and a long tail of regional brands. None of them ship anything in the photon-density range we operate in.

The defensible gap is not photon density alone — an incumbent could in principle stack more diodes. It is the combination of per-cell continuous-spectrum tuning with a recipe library validated against 60+ crop-stage combinations. Catching us requires both a hardware platform shift and three years of crop-science data they have not collected.
